<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Blutfresser

> Wicked being instinctively avoid it, a finality to all that isn't pure. A murderous intention too palpable to ignore despite its silent, ignore its stare and you will be its next victim.

A jagged, sharp, ornate, and fully metallic greatsword. In its brutalism architecture, it had hidden a machination only the mad could understand. It could siphon blood from his enemies, dealing damage and heals the wielder by **10%** of damage struck. Any enemy hit would cursed by **Crimspact Memento**.

On swing, a destructive path of **Shackling Doctrine** strike a way towards the target. The sword's thirst for blood is unquenchable, and its user is no different, it was truly a match made in hell. **5** seconds cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Abilities">

#### Bloody Mary

Ability to evade an attack and leaving a statue that explodes after **1.5** second, while also dashing backward. **5** seconds cooldown.

#### Brutal Pilgrimage

Ability to swiftly dash toward target, leaving trails of *Shackling Doctrine** strike on the path that causes **Crimspact Memento**. **2** seconds cooldown. Ignores range limit.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Passives">

#### Insatiable Devotion

Passive to regenerates **20%** of his lost health every **7** seconds. If no health is lost, will gain extra **100** of max health instead.

#### Righteous Tribute

Passive to convert every **2000** damage dealt into a **Shackling Doctrine** strike from random point towards self. Cooldown **1** seconds.

#### Heavy Wobble

Passive to simply move front and back slowly. It seems like he's impatient to get to the fight that even when he's idle he's already moving.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Crimspact Memento

Permanent effect that causes unit to lose their health slowly over time, slowing weapon and ability cooldown rate, while also reducing their max health accordingly to the current health.

</Tab>

<Accordion client:load title="Blutfresser?">

It was a reference to a weapon from **Across the Time 2**, A minecraft map by **Piccomaster**. In short, it's greatest asset is its ability to leech health from enemies, making it a formidable weapon in close combat, especially for a tanky character.

Since it was the one of the main weapon I used during playing the map, I decided to name the main weapon after it. A decrepit yet complex weapon that fits the theme of the character. In its lore it was written by the Inquisitor himself:

> **CHAPTER XI — DEVIL'S DEMISE** <br></br><br></br>THOU FORGED THING, HEWN OF FORGE AND FURROW, I RAISE MY CHOIR AGAINST THY ABYSSAL BREED, A SERMON CASTED IN WOUNDS! THOU ART A SERPENT OF STEEL, A CATHEDRAL OF RAZORS THAT MOCKS THE BEAST, SMITE, AND LET THE WICKED GROAN! CAST DOWN, O HELL, FOR THIS BLADE IS JUDGEMENT: BLOOD TRIBUTE, SIN TO FUEL, IT SHALL FEEDS ON THY OWN LIES! FLAMES THAT ANSWER THEE SHALL BE ASH UNDER ITS DEPRAVITY, SHALL IT SIPS THEIR LIFE AND TURNS THEIR WOE TO VICTORY. <br></br><br></br>WRETCHED SHALL BE THE HAND THAT HOLDS IT, FOR IT IS A PULPIT OF PUNISHMENT. TESTED ARE THOSE WHO DARES DENY ITS STARE, A TESTAMENT, NAILS OF FLESH, AND FLESH OF NAILS! STATUES OF REGRET! CHANT OF RUINATION! BY THIS DROUGHT IRON DOCTRINE, CURSED BOUND BY MEMORATION OF THE CRIMSON'S RESTRICTIVE LAW, NAMES REPUDIATED BEFORE RED DAWN! IT IS THE CHAIN THAT DRAGS DOWN HELL TO TASTE ITS OWN BILE. THY MACHINE SO CRUEL IN ITS REPRESSIVE TEARS, THUS HELL CRIES FOR MERCY.

~ Bloodfaith Testament 4:11

What it means in its actually? Possibly mad ramblings of a deranged mind, but it tells a bit about the weapon itself. It feeds on blood, and it punishes those who dare to oppose it. A weapon of judgment, a tool of retribution, and a symbol of the Inquisitor's punishment.

</Accordion>
