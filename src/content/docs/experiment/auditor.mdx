### Details

> You can't escape the equation.

Agent Googly, [**Auditor**]

The Equalizer, the Balancer and the Muscle for one of the experimentation branch teams: **E.X.P**.

After retirement, he was recalled one last time to make sure everything was in order. Once all archives had been checked and secured, *the Spider told him to shut the door, escape the realm, and issue authorization for the end sequence*.

#### Unstable Fist

> Tedious in theory, meticulous on practice

A long tie wrapped to a clenched fist, so condensed even the air around it implode. On swing, blink around enemy position then punch initiate. On successful impact, dealt exactly **1/10** damage to the target's max health, and dealt great knock. **IF ANY** of the target cost is bigger than **100000**, permanently enters an enraged version which instantly reduces cooldown upon usage. **4** seconds cooldown.

#### Imbalanced Scale

Passive ability to counter attack upon receiving damage, which push enemies and nullify damage taken, then dashes toward enemy position and reflect **50%** of the damage back to the damager. **IF ANY** of the damager unit has health beyond **100000**, permanently instantly refreshes ability upon use. Ignores distance, **5** seconds cooldown.

**THIS ABILITY ALSO SET USER HEALTH TO STATIC 500 ON START.**
