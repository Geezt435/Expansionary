### Details

> Your craftsmanship has grown lax.

Agent Hambody, [Artisan]

The Artist, the Magician, and the Containment Operator for one of the experimentation branch teams: **E.X.P**.

For his final designated task, he was given the directive to conjure a specific pattern from the center of the facility to the edge of each sector. Upon completion, he was ordered to abandon the realm and assume a new identity. *The Spider ensures that every crew member is dismissed when the operation reaches its final stage.*
