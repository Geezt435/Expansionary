### Details

> Infinite screens we all bore witness to, and only a precious few were worth keeping.

Agent Franz, [**Caretakerz**]

The Monitor, the Watcher, the Co-Leader for one of the experimentation branch teams: **E.X.P**.

When the end came, she requested permission from the leader to let her stay despite the finality requiring only one to remain, a burden the leader volunteered to carry. Not wanting the same tragedy to happen, *The Spider transported her to another realm by force, ensuring that he alone would remain to face the end*.
