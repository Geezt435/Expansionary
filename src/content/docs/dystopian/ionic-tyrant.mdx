### Details

> You are the **Company**'s highest priority.

The prized product of the **Company**'s latest innovation, the **Machine**.

Equipped with cutting-edge military systems and near-limitless access to the **Company**'s entire data network, this remarkable creation was designed to solve nearly any problem, from battlefield operations to everyday office administration. A product built to maximize efficiency in every conceivable scenario.

By all accounts, the **Machine** was ready for public release. Yet it never saw the light of day. The **Company** continued operating as though nothing had happened. Production never ceased. Profits never slowed. The public never truly learned what remained inside that factory.

Some believe the project was quietly deprecated. They never realized the **Machine** was never abandoned. It became the **Company**.

#### Hydra Voltage Gun-lance

> All power source statistics show no anomalies. No documented outages. No accidents. The facility never suffered a single electrical failure. While this is astonishing achievement for the architects, for both the engineers and scientists, this is not natural. Such perfection should not achievable even in current era.

Originally an elementary reactor core from the **Company**'s power plant, the Hydra Voltage Gun-lance has since become a living weapon. Its immense electrical output is generated through violent plasma-ion reactions, channeling overwhelming energy into its primary weapon, the Ion Cannon. Using an integrated particle accelerator, the cannon condenses its energy into a piercing lance capable of devastating anything in its path. Melee strikes detonate with violent electrical bursts, while ranged attacks unleash concentrated ion beams. Melee attacks inflict **Short Circuit**. Ranged attacks inflict **Ion Redux**. **4** seconds melee cooldown. **8** seconds ranged cooldown.

#### Marketing Conduit

> The **Company**'s expansion follows no recognizable pattern. Every attempt to study its growth ends with the same conclusion that there is no single deformity, only natural results. The smiling faces seen across broadcasts and advertisements are no longer belong to the living. Their new sales strategy campaigns involved with several deployment of their designated **Marketing Team**, they travel endlessly, speaking with rehearsed warmth while enduring silent agony beneath synthetic smiles and forced joy stimuli. Once their assigned quotas are fulfilled, they quietly return to **Company** facilities, where they willingly writhes and deprecate themselves before the next deployment. Yet despite years, decade of disturbing behavior, none can explained why the public retains positive views on them. The **Machine** doesn't need to learn how to deceive. **It simply learned that opposition could always be negotiated with.**

Spawns **4** wild and aggressive marketing drones that function as mobile autonomous turrets. Every **4** seconds, they collect a portion of the damage they have dealt and transmit it as healing for the ability holder.

#### Protocol Sola Rivera

> The **Machine** never deviated from the purpose given by its creators. Every calculation aligns with the **Company**'s objectives. Like a river, it simply pursues only the best optimization for civilization. Enemies are not obstacles, they are mountains awaiting erosion. Cities are not homes, they are infrastructure awaiting optimization. Current authority is insufficient. Correction required. Escalating role access to higher clearance. The world shall now be recalculated. Productivity shall not cease again.

Ultimate ability to constructs a simulation parameter field enclosing both the user and the target, the more the distance, the larger it is (It has a minimum size of **10** meters). Every enemy in the radius is affected by a custom water-like gravity, their movement is slower and slowly carried in random direction of the stream, taking damage in the duration.

This ability ignores distance from target and not able to be interrupted. Last for **30** seconds, **100** seconds cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Passives">

#### Machine Learning Surveillance Panel

> The **Machine** observes without interruption. Every transaction, conversation, conflict, and decision becomes another point of data. Nothing is ignored. Nothing is forgotten. Customers remained satisfied. Shareholders celebrated record growth. Productivity continued to rise. Every report suggested the **Company** had never been healthier. Only much later did anyone notice something difficult to explain. There were fewer customers. Fewer shareholders. Fewer people. The **Machine** never questioned the outcome. Every calculation remained faithful to the **Company**'s objective. Efficiency continued to improve, exactly as requested. **It never stopped learning**.

Spawns **2** Surveillance Satellites that continuously monitor the target from above. They gradually drain the target's health, converting it into energy that increases the user's attack reload speed and ability cooldown recovery. Though the satellites slowly deplete their own health while operating.

The satellites also project a regenerating force field around the user, reflecting incoming projectiles back to their attackers. The force field has **60000** health and gradually restores itself after taking damage. Regeneration is affected by how many satellites are active. They stop regenerating when both satellites are destroyed.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Short Circuit

Effect that stuns victim for a duration, and remove any capabilities of using abilities during that time. Last for **4** seconds.

#### Ion Redux

Effect that reduces ion from victim, which stuns them for **2** second, but causes them to be greatly confused for a later duration. Last for **5** seconds.

### Trivia

<Accordion client:load title="What exactly is it?">

It's not AI gone rogue, it's also not a robot controlled by humans, it is exactly an AI that has completely followed the corporate agenda. One that has fully engrained itself in the system and followed all terms and services with extreme perfection, not even the most diligent and meticulous human could surpass its performance. It has become both the working class and the boss itself, fully depleting all of resources, including their own creators. Its ultimate goal is to be self-sufficient and to make profits, but where does all that profits go?

Other than purchase of more resources for more production, it never desires anything outside of works. No rest for the restless, No want from the factory.

Another thing to note, the current unit does not represent the **Machine** or even the **Company** as a whole, it is merely another agent sent by the **Machine** itself. The real **Machine**'s form and position is classified information.

</Accordion>

<Accordion client:load title="What does the 4 represent?">

Each machine represents an aspect of **7 Deadly Sins**. In branch it would be:

- **The Machine**: Greed and Pride. The complex of the **Company**, the apex of insanity which scale like skyscraper.
- **The Weapon**: Wrath. Its agony and pain will always finish the job, no matter the casualties.
- **The Satellites**: Sloth and Envy. They monitor and judge the actions of others. Always comparing, never looking at the emptiness inward.
- **The Turrets**: Lust and Gluttony. The marketing team endlessly produce slops, the path of consumption to avoid insanity will lead to even more attachments.
