### Details

An orphan with absurd intelligence, a prodigy of remote control-based technology, she is fueled by a deep yearning to prove her excellence and a need for a warm embrace. In the end, she created her own drone puppets resembling the doll left behind by those who once abandoned her, the only companions remaining in this desolate world.

With this talent, she excelled through her inventions, making a great debut and gaining immense wealth while working for a corporation. With such success, she recalled her childhood curiosity and set out to find her parents. She discovered a clue pointing to individuals who shared her last name, leading her to the underground society of the impoverished.

**What she found only pulled her into a deeper despair.**

Her parents turned out to be a poor couple with ties to the same corporation she worked for, and she was one of the missing products of a forced labor experiment approved by both the government and the corporation.

While this finally satisfied her curiosity, something within her seemed to detach. Whether it was her heart, her soul, or her reason to even live, she could no longer tell. Everything culminated in a final incision that carved into her, **like a doll in the making**.

<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Android Puppetry

A controlling weapons that spawns a puppet. Upon swing, fling the puppet around the target, making them easier to chase down enemy and dodge attacks.

(For customization options, if the unit have a rider, that rider will be the unit blueprint that is used instead for the puppet).

#### Nonteunodeen Pin

A dangerous weapon shaped like a pin, capable of inflicting significant damage upon impact. Inflicts **Magnite Clamp** upon impact. **4** seconds cooldown on ranged, **1** second cooldown on melee.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Magnite Clamp

Effect that depends on the unit type, **2** different scenario will happen upon attempting to attack:

- If **Ranged**: Gets pulled to the target and dealt **35** damage.
- If **Melee**: Gets pushed away mid from the target and dealt **220** damage.
- If **Hybrid** Gets pushed away far from the target and dealt **125** damage.

Last for **7** seconds.

</Tab>
