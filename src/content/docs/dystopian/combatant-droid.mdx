### Details

A robot designed generally to handle riots, it bashed and pushed away oppositions forcefully. Many tax money is used to maintain and deploy these units, ironically.

### Capabilities

<Tabs client:load>

<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Riot Metal Shield

A metal shield great for the use of holding back riots. **3** seconds cooldown.

#### Tazing Baton

An electric metal baton, can be charged to inflicts the **Tazed** effect. Recharging time is **4** seconds. Attack time cooldown is **1** second.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Tazed

A status effect that temporarily paralyzes the target, preventing them from taking any actions. Last for **2** seconds.

</Tab>

</Tabs>
