### Details

A genius scientist with doctorate degrees in both biology and chemistry. Sadly, all that learning and knowledge brought him no funding, no wealth, and no support, only replacement by corporate automatons churning out mass-produced products that barely met minimum standards. Consumed by depression, insanity, and encroaching despair, he one day forsook his humanity. He ceased caring for his impoverished circumstances and resorted to depraved experiments upon the underbelly of society. He believed it was all in pursuit of discovery.

**The growing number of missing persons whispered otherwise.**
