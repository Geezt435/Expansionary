### Details

A classified man-beast specimen clad in draconic armor reminiscent of a wild beast, wielding a blade seemingly forged to slay one. Its release into the outside world was deemed an accident, though the company continues to deny any responsibility.

### Capabilities

<Tabs client:load>

<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Positron Longsword

> Noise of both the matter and anti-matter. Like a man shouts to the beast.

A glitched and unstable saber from experimental arsenal. Wielding it can expose one to electrical surges and instability. Merely touching its edge can result in possible death. **2** seconds cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Abilities">

#### Holographic Infectoid

> Diagnosed Prediction Finalized. Restrains Released. Executing **H.I**.

Ultimate ability to execute **20** consecutive slices against closest targets, bringing them up with slash on top and downward thrust on the final cut. Basic damage base follows weapon damage, and the final downward thrust deals triple damage. Minimum damage is **200**. **20** seconds cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Passives">

#### Antivirus

Passive ability to reduce any incoming damage for **4** second, which slowly decreases over time. If any damage is taken during this period, the damage reduction will be applied based on how seconds have passed. Maximum decreased damage is **90%** and minimum is **20%**. Damage reduction is ignored if the damage type is piercing.

</Tab>

</Tabs>
