### Details

A standard reconnaissance drone deployed by the **Company** for surveying and intelligence-gathering purposes. Its arrival signifies that the area has been selected for *evaluation process*. By the time further assets are dispatched, decisions regarding its future have often already been finalized.

### Capabilities

<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Cyber Surveillance

A screening device that scan for threats. Upon finding a threat, it scans the area, after **1** second delay, sends a shockwave that inflicts **Blinded** effect. **12** seconds cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Blinded

A blinding effect that blinds target, causing them to stumble and walk in random direction. Last for **5** seconds.
