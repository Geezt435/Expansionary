### Details

Ghost and phantom entities lack scientific validation, until a certain researcher devised a way to establish communication using empirical method and alienating technologies with the *ecto-realm*. The consequence of this discovery was trade of souls, his soul in return of her long lost daughter.

Nonetheless, he regarded this bargain as a gift rather than a curse, as it gives his daughter a future other than grim despair of death. Her father musical harmonies resonating through the cold tranquil night.

<Tab name="Main Attacks">

#### Remix Encore Instrument

> An instrumental combination electric guitar, upon listening to it, one can hear the echo chambered within. Mastery over it would mean the tranquility of both noise and static, and finality to compose a song to shatter the veil of the world.

Has **2** usage depending on the target's health or user health:

* **If above 50% health**: Enters a passive state, the user only bops their own head, while dashing around and toward target.
* **If below 50% health**: Enters an aggressive state, the user will actively deals damage and life steal from target's current health.

Only up to **10** amount of **Echo** can be spawned, if more is spawned, all of them explodes. **1** second weapon cooldown.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Passives">

#### Discordance Resonance

Passive to have **4** speaker behind her.

Ultimate ability to tune up her music box to let out a series of cacophony beats which causes a **Reverb Seizure** effect on her nearby enemies. This makes her runs faster and also completely invulnerable in the process.

Ability to summon **4** speakers that faced outward around her. Upon any attack damage taken, the user will enter an invulnerability state. Depending on the situation, the user may also gain additional benefits.

</Tab>

<Tab name="Effects">

#### Reverb Seizure

Effect that does small damage to victim overtime, while also makes victim bop their heads up and down, disrupting their movement and attacks. Also makes unit slightly confused. Last for **1.5** seconds.

At the end of the duration, explodes into a cloud of musical notes that inflicts the same effects to the other units in the area.

</Tab>

<Tab name="On Death">

#### Final Melody

On death, explodes into a large musical explosion that inflicts small damage and causes **Reverb Seizure**.

</Tab>
